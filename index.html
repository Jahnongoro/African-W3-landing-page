Who We Are

Africa Web3 Institute (AWI) sits at the intersection of technology, policy, and society.

We translate complex emerging technologies into clear policy insights, practical education, and actionable frameworks for Africa’s digital economy.

From regulators to builders, from classrooms to cabinet rooms — we shape conversations that matter.

Africa is not catching up — it is building forward.

Web3 presents a once-in-a-generation opportunity to redesign financial systems, governance models, and digital ownership on African terms.

We exist to ensure Africa leads responsibly, inclusively, and intelligently.
